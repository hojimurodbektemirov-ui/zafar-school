Parents ask, we answer

Pick a question and the answer arrives just like in our contact centre, @ZISaloqa.

Are there separate English and Russian classes?
No. In grades 1–8 every student studies English and Russian equally. No class or student is set apart.
What time do lessons start and finish?
On the winter schedule lessons start at 08:30 and children go home at 16:00. Breakfast is served until 08:25. Saturday is a day off for students and a training day for teachers.
What if my child doesn't pass the test?
There are three attempts at the academic test. The next attempt comes after preparation, no sooner than 5 days later. Candidates below the grant threshold can still be admitted if places are available.
How can I follow my child's progress?
Every class has a private group with the timetable, announcements, photos and videos. Questions about grades, attendance, payments and teacher meetings go to one contact centre, @ZISaloqa.
Is there a school bus?
Yes, air-conditioned school buses. The service is paid. Seats are limited, so families living far away and younger students come first.
How are meals organised?
Three times a day: breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack. The menu is balanced and sometimes chosen by the children together with teachers.
Do we need to buy textbooks?
No. In the 2026–2027 school year the school provides textbooks for every grade.
Can children bring phones to school?
No. Students come to school without phones so their attention stays on the lesson. The rule matters most in the upper grades.
Can I see the school beforehand?
Of course. We hold regular open days with a tour, an open lesson, time with teachers and a food tasting. You can also arrange a visit on any other day.
